  1. 01

    Download

    Get the latest release ZIP. Close the game.

  2. 02

    Place

    Put SaureksCloset in Interface/AddOns/ and SaureksCloset.dll beside WoW.exe.

  3. 03

    Enter

    Add the DLL to dlls.txt after VanillaHelpers.dll. Launch with VanillaFixes and type /closet.
